A side-scrolling sword fighter drawn entirely in text characters. Each screen below is rendered live by the same character-grid engine the game will use, so what you see here is what ships. Switch palette and rendering mode to compare directions.

What to lock in

  1. Palette. Dusk (full color), Sumi-e (ink on paper, one red), or Phosphor (green CRT).
  2. Rendering. Rich cells uses background colors and sub-cell blocks for smooth ridges and bars. Pure ASCII sticks to the 95 printable characters.
  3. Sprite scale. The fighters are about 15 rows tall. Bigger means more detail and fewer enemies on screen.
  4. Effects level. Arcs, sparks, afterimages and focus lines can be toned down if the screen feels busy.
  5. Platform. Browser (this engine, 60 fps canvas) or a real terminal build. The art direction carries over either way.
